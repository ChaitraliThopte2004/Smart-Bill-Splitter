About Smart Bill Splitter

Smart Bill Splitter is designed to help people manage their money easily and smartly. Whether you are a student, working professional, or managing a household, this platform allows you to track expenses, manage subscriptions, and split bills.

Our Mission

To make expense tracking simple, stress-free and accessible to everyone.

What You Can Do Here

  • ✔ Track your daily expenses
  • ✔ View spending category-wise
  • ✔ Track subscriptions & bill reminders
  • ✔ Split bills fairly
  • ✔ See monthly and daily totals

Why We Built This

Many people lose track of where their money goes. Smart Bill Splitter lets you stay organized and financially aware.
